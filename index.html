For Parents

What this is

Steady Ground is a learning partner for children who find schoolwork hard to get into — not a homework machine. Every tab is built to help a child understand something, get started on it, or calm down enough to try. It will not write essays, answer worksheet questions, or solve maths problems, and it is instructed to refuse when asked.

Where your child's words go

Nowhere. The whole helper runs inside this browser, on this device. There is no account, no server, and no internet connection needed once it has downloaded. Nothing typed here is saved, uploaded, or counted — close the tab and it is genuinely gone. The only things stored on this device are four settings: night mode, text size, whether the welcome guide has been seen, and whether the helper has already been downloaded.

The safety filters, honestly

The helper is a small model. It is much less capable than the big AI assistants you may have used, which means it can also be less predictable, so this app checks its work rather than trusting it. Everything a child types is checked before it is sent, and everything that comes back is checked again before it is shown — for language, for coherence, and for answers that have wandered off. When a check fails, your child sees a friendly "let's try that again" message instead of whatever the model produced.

No filter is perfect. A determined older child can find ways around any filter that runs on their own device, and a small model will sometimes give an answer that is simply unhelpful or a bit wrong. Please treat this as a tool your child uses near you, not one you hand over and walk away from — especially in the first few weeks. Sitting with them the first few times is worth more than any of the filtering above.

If your child types something worrying

If the app detects that a child may be in distress — anything about hurting themselves, or being hurt by someone — it does not send that to the model at all. It shows a fixed, human-written message encouraging them to talk to a trusted adult, along with helpline numbers. It cannot alert you, because it keeps no record of anything. If you want to know what your child has been asking, ask them, and use the 🚩 Looks wrong button together — it copies an exchange to the clipboard so you can look at it side by side.

It is not a therapist or a doctor

Calm Corner and What Can I Try offer grounding exercises and activity ideas. They are not treatment, diagnosis, or advice, and the helper is told never to offer medical or psychological advice.
